# Signed sessions over bounded native foundations

One node owns Babel, complete-manifest gossip, derived service metadata and RAM
certificate relays across authenticated carrier sessions. Heavy operations lend
an immutable context to the serialized crypto worker; no independent service
gossip or signature exists. C5 retains full blobs, C6 retains verified compact
endpoint projections and delegates its sole next hop. Device persistence
callbacks are absent; peer inputs remain in bounded RAM. The later actor and
VIP layers provide actual radio and application effects. This layer activates
no public native runtime and excludes future operator-origin authorization.
